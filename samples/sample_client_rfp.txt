Project RFP: On-Demand Mobile Mechanics Marketplace (Client Brief)

Overview:
We are looking for an agile full-stack engineer to build an on-demand booking application for mobile auto mechanics.
The app needs to be blazing fast, modern, and extremely user-friendly.

Scope of Work:
1. Interactive customer booking portal and mechanic dispatch dashboard.
2. Integration with various other APIs, SMS gateways, and payment processors.
3. Mobile-friendly responsive layouts, progressive web app features, etc.
4. Real-time GPS mechanic tracking and status notifications.

Project Terms & Expectations:
- We expect contractor to be available as needed throughout the launch cycle.
- Unlimited revisions until fully satisfied by our product advisory team.
- Payment upon completion of the entire application on net-60 terms.
- Developer must provide 180 days of free maintenance and bug-fixing after release.
- Developer agrees to full indemnification and unlimited liability against any software failures or downtime.
